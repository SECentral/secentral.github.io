Less noise. More context.

An investigation starts
where the logs leave off.

Bring SELinux evidence together across your hosts, without losing the details that make each denial worth understanding.

01 / ORGANIZE

Grouped issues. Preserved evidence.

Group related denials into one issue while keeping each occurrence and its original audit evidence available for investigation.

02 / INVESTIGATE

Follow the evidence.

Explore original audit records, observed context, and evidence-backed guidance. Similar denials can have different causes on different hosts.

03 / OBSERVE

Know your hosts.

See agent reporting state, SELinux mode, and observed host configuration in one place. Keep freshness and missing observations in view.

04 / COMPARE

Understand differences.

Explore observed configuration drift and compare supported settings with applicable policy catalog references. A difference is a lead, not a verdict.

05 / EXPLORE

Keep the event trail.

Browse the centralized SELinux log alongside grouped issues. Inspect activity across hosts and return to the underlying audit evidence.

06 / OWN

Your data. Your decisions.

Review evidence and resolution options without treating audit2allow output as proof that access should be allowed. SECentral does not automatically deploy policy changes.
